    I'm a Computer Engineer working at the intersection of artificial intelligence and music.

    After graduating in Computer Engineering from Ankara University (GPA 3.5/4.0), I'm now pursuing a Master's in Sound and Music Computing at Universitat Pompeu Fabra in Barcelona.

    My research centers on machine learning for music and sound — including music source separation, cover song identification, music pattern discovery, and genre classification — with a particular focus on representation learning and similarity modeling for music analysis and generation.

    My main focus right now is SmartSynthesizer: an AI-driven system that learns the parameters behind a sound and turns deep-learning research into a real, playable audio plugin — bridging neural networks and music production.

    Beyond research, I'm a musician. I wrote, recorded, and produced Kara Pehlivan, an 8-track Turkish rock album, together with a team of 10+ collaborators. In 2022, we co-founded Shall We Entertainment Student Community, which I led as President until 2026, growing it to 400+ members across 12 universities and organizing 40+ events.
